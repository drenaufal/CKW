## Company Overview

PT. Cempaga Karya Wijaya is a modern silica sand processing company established to serve the growing demand for premium quality silica sand throughout Indonesia and international markets.

Located strategically in Rembang, Central Java, our newly constructed processing plant combines advanced washing, purification, blending, and sizing technology with an efficient logistics network to deliver high-quality silica sand products for various industrial applications.

Our plant has been established by an experienced management team with extensive expertise in silica mining, mineral processing, domestic marketing, and international exports. This combination of technical knowledge and operational experience enables us to consistently supply products that meet the quality requirements of demanding industries.

---

## Strategic Location

PT. Cempaga Karya Wijaya enjoys a highly advantageous location that provides significant operational and logistical benefits.

The processing plant is situated:
- Adjacent to the four-lane national highway within Rembang City.
- Approximately 10 km from Rembang Port.
- Conveniently positioned between the major industrial corridors of Semarang and Surabaya.

This strategic location enables efficient transportation of raw materials from Central Kalimantan while providing economical distribution of finished silica sand products to customers throughout Java and overseas export destinations.

The close proximity to Rembang Port significantly reduces transportation costs for both inbound raw materials and outbound export shipments.

---

## Our Operations

The primary objective of PT. Cempaga Karya Wijaya is to produce consistent, high-quality silica sand products through advanced processing techniques.

Our process includes:
- Blending premium silica sand sourced from Central Kalimantan with selected local silica sand from the Rembang region.
- Washing to remove impurities.
- Purification to achieve higher silica quality.
- Precise sizing and grading according to customer specifications.
- Quality control throughout every stage of production.
- Delivery of finished products directly to customers.

The company maintains a long-term raw material supply agreement with PT. Cempaga Surya Timur, a reputable silica sand mining company in Central Kalimantan. The owners of PT. Cempaga Surya Timur are also shareholders in PT. Cempaga Karya Wijaya, ensuring secure, reliable, and sustainable access to premium silica sand resources.
